## Strategy Analysis

### 1. Covered Call ETF Portfolio (Permission To Be Wealthy) — *income investing, newly graduated theme*

- **Model:** Education channel + stock-analysis.com affiliate; her own covered-call ETF portfolio as on-camera proof. Claims "$4,000+ consistently per month" since 2023 (implies ~$350–500K invested).
- **The screen:** monthly payers, ≥10% distribution rate, ≥15% 1-yr total return, positive 1-yr price return. Layers: TSPY core (14.7% dist, 0DTE on 100%), QQQI/GPIQ tech, IWMI small-cap, IGLD gold, YieldMax-style funds satellite-only.
- **Automation:** 4/10 (screening is scriptable; it's passive investing, not an operable business) · **Quality:** 6/10 · **Verdict: Mixed**
- **Receipts test:** fails — $4K/mo is a verbal claim, no brokerage dashboard. "No NAV erosion" judged on a 1-year bull-window proxy; GDXY's 100%+ distribution rate is a textbook NAV-decay signature; several funds <2 years old, untested through a drawdown.
- **Key actions:** if pursuing, rebuild the screen with multi-cycle data; prioritize ROC/tax-efficient funds in taxable accounts; cap YieldMax-style funds at satellite size; treat distributions as partial ROC until a fund survives a correction.
- **Red flags:** equity risk repackaged as yield; affiliate code monetizes the recommendation; young funds showcased as proven.
