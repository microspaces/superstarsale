## Strategy Analysis

### 1. Print on Demand With a LIVE Store (Meg Heckman) — *POD / owned brand*

- **Model:** Owned-store POD brand (Sloth Hiking Club, hiking niche) on Printful, $34.95 Comfort Colors 1717, FB ads + owned email list. Anti-marketplace thesis: own the customer (Etsy forbids email contact).
- **Numbers:** unit math on screen — $34.95 − $15.47 fulfillment ≈ $19–20 gross/order, 15–20% net after ads; 71 orders the day of filming; sister brand $600K (2023) → $1.8M (2025); unprofitable first two months, stated plainly.
- **Automation:** 6/10 (fulfillment 100% outsourced; design iteration + ad management are the skilled manual core) · **Quality:** 8/10 — the receipts-test passes: named store, live order count, complete unit math, honest loss period · **Verdict: Worth Trying**
- **Key actions:** ad cost under ~$10/order is the whole game at these margins; owned store + email capture from day one; test identity-humor design batches; budget 2 months of losses before judging.
- **Red flags:** 15–20% net leaves zero room for ad-skill mistakes; the $1.8M sister brand is unnamed/unverifiable; free-course funnel top; survivorship in the growth story.

### 2. SBA Loan Business Acquisition (Michael S Glaspie) — *acquisitions / financing*

- **Model:** Buy cash-flowing businesses with SBA-guaranteed loans instead of growing organically; bookkeeping-channel creator, so bank-ready financials as the qualification keystone.
- **Deal structure:** SBA caps $5M, lends 90% LTV → $1M business needs $100K equity; seller-carry on half the equity slice → ~5% cash to close. ~prime+1.75–2 pts (≈9.5–10%), 60–90-day approvals.
- **New mechanic vs the archive's acquisition coverage (search funds, newsletter roll-ups, equity-for-services):** the **100%-financing expansion loan** — 2+ years operating a same-NAICS business unlocks SBA funding for target + building + working capital at closing.
- **Automation:** 2/10 · **Quality:** 6/10 — loan guidelines are real public facts, the NAICS trick is legitimate policy, but his personal deal is asserted with zero artifacts · **Verdict: Mixed**
- **Key actions:** screen acquisitions for NAICS-code matches if you have a 2+ year operating business; underwrite at ~10% cost of capital first; negotiate seller financing on the equity slice into every LOI.
- **Red flags:** "cash in my pocket" is clickbait; the 100% path disqualifies most viewers; ~10% money is expensive; bookkeeping-content funnel ending.
