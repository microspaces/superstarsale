  • Demoed workflow: incoming reseller email for 100 units → pulls pricing from Google Sheet → builds quotation PDF in QuickBooks → pastes back into email draft → follows up the order. "Email to Google Sheet to QuickBooks back to email" — full business loop closed.

  • Koerner's framing: 200–300 ads running on Meta, no human can track CPA/CTR per ad — Violoop builds consolidated tracking dashboards and generates replacement creatives.

Those receipts check out as working software. But every headline number — 2 hrs/day saved, 16–30 hrs of output, GPT-3.5/4 benchmark parity, 20–30 step roadmap — is vendor self-report with zero data shown. The "100 units quotation" deal is anecdote, not evidence.

Verdict: Mixed — 5/10. The always-on screen-context idea is genuinely differentiated from chat-with-a-browser tools, and the live demo shows real working capability. But it's a promotional interview where every quantified benefit is the founder's own claim, the hardware-local model will lag frontier cloud models (admitted in-video), and the whole thing funnels to a Kickstarter purchase decision. Interesting to watch; not enough evidence to buy hardware on.
