Key Takeaways

1. The best model today: Micro-SaaS. Angus Chang's story is the most replicable and has the clearest path from $0 to $40K MRR. The key is solving a painful, specific problem and having 2-3 years of runway.

2. High-ticket services are underrated. Hormozi's framework of starting with expensive 1-on-1 work is counterintuitive but powerful. It funds everything else and gives you unmatched customer insight.

3. Physical automation is surprisingly accessible. ATMs and vending machines aren't sexy, but they generate real cash flow with minimal time investment. Good for diversifying beyond digital.

4. Avoid oversold "beginner friendly" models. Iman's growth marketing pitch is mostly a funnel into a $5K course. The model works, but not the way he frames it.

5. The passive income stack is saturated. Greg's KDP/Merch/YouTube combo was great in 2018-2022. In 2026, these markets are extremely competitive. YouTube still works, but the barrier to entry is much higher.
