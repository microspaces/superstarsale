## Video 2: How I Scaled a New Etsy Shop to $91,894.12 in 3 Months — Taylor Posada

**Business model:** Etsy print-on-demand (Printify) shop, single niche (events/party), signs → expanded product types; data-driven listing + Etsy ads strategy. She also sells a course/community (Plan Scale Profit / POD Connection).

**Revenue model:** Dashboard-shown: Oct 194 orders >$6K; Nov 2,000+ orders; YTD operating profit $29,997.79 at 27.6% margin (implies ~$108K revenue; headline is revenue, not profit). Ad spend quoted $675 vs $200-300/day budget — inconsistent.

**Replication steps:**
1. Pick broad year-round niche.
2. One product type, 5-10 unique listings/wk.
3. Run Etsy ads for data, not sales.
4. Double down on winning designs: same SEO, new products.
5. Expand winning design across product types to lift AOV ($50-60).

**Automation potential: 6/10** — design gen, SEO tags, listing copy, mockups all automatable; ad monitoring + trend taste semi-automatable.

**Quality: 6/10** — real ProfitTree dashboards and cost breakdown (COGS $43K, fees), but full-time experienced seller with a course to sell; outcome explicitly "not normal."

**Verdict: Mixed** — method is sound and receipts exist; the headline result is survivorship.

**Key actions:**
- Launch one-niche POD shop.
- Batch-generate 30 listings.
- Treat ads as research spend with weekly double-down rule.

**Red flags:** Course-seller incentive; "one listing away from life-changing" framing; margin below her usual 30-35%.

## Video 4: How to Sell AI Agents to Local Businesses In 2026 — Mikey No Code

**Business model:** Productized agency selling AI receptionist agents (Base44 "Super Agent") to local businesses (HVAC, plumbing, dental, real estate): instant replies, lead qualification, calendar booking via Telegram/WhatsApp/email. Creator's actual revenue: free Base44 masterclass funnel.

**Revenue model:** Suggested pricing — "$2–5,000" setup + "$300–800/mo" management + optional per-appointment/per-lead fees. Ranges, not receipts.

**Replication steps:**
1. Pick ONE local industry, ideally one you have a contact in.
2. Build demo agent in Base44: persona prompt, FAQ/services/pricing into memory, connect calendar + Telegram (BotFather token).
3. Set pricing: setup + retainer (+ optional performance).
4. Prospect via Google My Business listings for niche + city.
5. Outreach: short personalized demo offer; 2-3 follow-ups across channels.
6. Sales call: discovery → live demo (agent books on screen) → close to small starter setup.
7. Onboard: clone agent with client's voice/FAQ, connect their calendar/Gmail/Telegram, monitor and refine.

**Automation potential: 9/10** — the product IS automation; delivery is template-clone per client; even outreach could be agent-driven. Only trust-building calls stay human.

**Quality: 4/10** — coherent, actionable playbook, but zero named clients or dashboards; numbers are "ranges that work" and the video funnels into a free masterclass. Missed-call ROI framing is plausible but unproven.

**Verdict: Mixed**

**Key actions:**
- Build one vertical-specific demo (e.g., plumbing after-hours booking) before outreach.
- Scrape 50 GMB listings in one niche; run 3-touch demo-offer sequences.
- Price low for first 2-3 case studies, then raise to setup + retainer.

**Red flags:** No named clients or verified revenue; masterclass funnel motive; every viewer gets the same crowded-agency playbook; assumes businesses grant messaging/calendar access.

1. **Vertical AI-agent agencies remain the highest-automation play** — setup fee ($2-5K) + retainer ($300-800/mo), near-total delivery automation via template cloning. No new evidence of verified client revenue yet; demand Stripe/dashboard proof before believing the math.
2. **Execution leverage is the real signal today:** Claude Code-style agents compressing production labor (batch POD design variants; per-client agent cloning) appeared in two separate videos — a tactic worth porting, even though the business models themselves are already covered.
3. **POD receipts beat POD claims:** Video 2 showed real dashboards (27.6% margin, $30K operating profit) while Videos 1/3 gave unverifiable dollar claims. When POD comes up, trust dashboards, ignore "$/day" headlines.
4. **No new business models graduated today** — all five videos mapped to known themes (agencies, AI services, ecommerce/POD, creator monetization, course funnels, cold outreach).
5. **Quality was low overall (avg 3.8/10):** one operator with receipts, three course-funnel plays, one straight-up ad. Skip-tier day for new model discovery.
