### 3. Personalized Jewelry Dropshipping — Custom Name Necklaces via Wix + Alibaba (Odetta Rockhead)

**Business model:** No-inventory dropshipping of personalized products (custom name / music-theme jewelry). Storefront on Wix with a .store domain; suppliers on Alibaba manufacture and ship the engraved item directly to the buyer. Case study: "Anna's" store allegedly sold 303,000+ units over 3 years (~8,400/month) at ~$14.10 AOV ≈ $118K/month revenue; 30% margin used to justify the $35K/month profit headline. Video is also a funnel for .store domains.

**Revenue model:**
- Markup on personalized jewelry (~$14–$20 retail vs. cheap Alibaba blanks)
- Volume at low AOV: thousands of orders/month required to hit the headline number
- No inventory carrying cost; supplier ships direct
- Margin compressed by ads, payment fees, returns, and shipping delays on custom goods

**Steps to replicate:**
1. Stand up a Wix (or Shopify) store with a professional .store domain.
2. Source customizable jewelry suppliers on Alibaba; confirm engraving/personalization SLAs.
3. List products with personalization fields (name, date, song).
4. Forward each order + custom text to the supplier (manual at first; automate via Zapier/Make or native integration).
5. Buy traffic (ads/SEO/social); scale only after supplier reliability is proven.

**Automation potential: 6/10** — Order forwarding, store ops, and creative production are automatable. Personalized SKUs add a custom-text hop that generic POD does not have. Ads, supplier QA, and refunds remain human. Unlike KDP, there is no marketplace demand engine — you must buy or earn every visitor.

**Quality score: 4/10** — The math is an illustrative case study (one store, averaged over 3 years, 30% margin assumed, no P&L). Channel is a side-hustle / mindset funnel with a domain affiliate. Personalized dropshipping is a real model, but 2026 ad costs, Alibaba quality variance, and custom-goods return rates make $35K/month an outlier, not a template.

**Verdict: Mixed** — Operationally systemizable, but the revenue event depends on paid traffic and a fragile supplier. Prefer Amazon POD (Gottfried #1) where demand + fulfillment are native. If testing dropship, start with 1 SKU, 50 orders, and measured CAC before scaling.

**Key action items:**
- If testing: one personalized SKU, Alibaba sample order, 50 paid-traffic orders, kill if CAC > 30% of AOV.
- Automate order-forward (custom text → supplier) via Zapier/Make before adding SKUs.
- Do not chase the $35K case study; treat it as a ceiling illustration, not a forecast.
