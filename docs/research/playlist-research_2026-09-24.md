**Receipts shown vs self-reported:** Almost entirely self-reported. "Hundreds of thousands of dollars" — no proof. - Free tier with ads (AdSense) can monetize even non-paying users
- Software products are sellable assets ($50K–$1M exits) vs un-sellable one-off digital files
- Charge against the cost of the human alternative (wedding planner $$$) not against competing spreadsheets
