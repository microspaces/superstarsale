## Key Takeaways

1. **Micro-SaaS is the ultimate automation play** — Build once, earn forever. The Bank Statement Converter story proves you don't need VC funding, a team, or social media fame. Just solve one painful problem really well.

2. **Systems > Hustle** — Every successful business in today's research runs on systems, not founder hours. The question isn't "how hard can I work?" but "how can I make this run without me?"

3. **Start with what you can afford** — Harry's journey from $3K ATM to self-storage empire shows the power of stacking. Don't wait for the perfect business. Start with the possible one.

4. **Charge more, sell less** — Ali Abdaal's $2,000 rule is crucial. You don't need 1,000 customers at $10. You need 5 customers at $2,000. This changes everything about how you build.

5. **Ignore social media (for the business)** — Angus explicitly says social media is a waste of time for his SaaS. Focus on the product, not the personal brand.
