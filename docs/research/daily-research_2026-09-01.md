## Strategy Analysis

### 1. Dan Martell — The Only 14 Ways to Make Money with AI in 2026

**Business model:** A tier-ranked catalog (S/A/B/F) of 14 AI business models. The money quotes: **AI agent development** is his "S of the S of the S" top pick — building multi-agent systems (chief-of-staff agent + sales bots + document processors) sold to SMBs. Ranked below it: **managed AI cybersecurity** (S tier), **AI consulting** ($5k audit → $50k implementation, S tier), **AI lead generation** (S tier), **AI voice agents** ($500–$1,500/mo retainers for missed-call businesses, A tier), **AI-to-AI marketplaces** (top of A). He explicitly F-tiers faceless AI YouTube ("AI slop... bottom feeder of the internet") and AI trading bots.

**How money is made:** Agent development: retainers/build fees attacking SMB labor dollars — replace a person and "$500–$1,500/month feels cheap." Consulting: $5k audits upsold to ~$50k implementations. Cybersecurity: enterprise-level monthly contracts selling 24/7 AI threat management.

**Automation score: 9/10** — the delivery is literally software agents; his own 5-step launch path (validate via landing page → pre-sell waitlist → launch manually/concierge → recruit distribution partners → productize last) is itself a systematizable playbook. Sales remains manual, delivery is code.

**Quality score: 8/10** — Martell launches an AI company monthly (SaaS Academy/Clarity.fm track record), gives concrete pricing, and usefully names crowded-vs-underserved gaps (everyone builds voice agents; almost nobody can sell them; SMB adoption is still near zero). Deductions: plugs his Apex platform and Instagram DM funnel throughout.

**Steps to replicate (his own blueprint for the #1 pick):**
1. Landing page for ONE use case — "AI chief of staff" (email, calendar, follow-ups; 10–15 hrs/week back)
2. Text everyone: "Who do you know that's looking for an AI-powered chief of staff?" (ask for referrals, not the sale)
3. Ask if they'd pay to solve it today → pre-sell an early-adoer program via payment link before building
4. Launch manually (concierge/custom) — prove real transactions before automating
5. Recruit partners already discussing the problem; pay them per sale with tracked links
6. Only then productize and reinvest income into the build

**Verdict: Worth Trying** — AI agent development for a single vertical is the clearest high-automation play in today's set; the validate→pre-sell→manual→partner→productize sequence is the reusable meta-playbook.

**Key action items:** Pick one vertical + one agent use case (chief of staff or after-hours voice agent); ship the landing page this week; run the referral-blast script; pre-sell 3 concierge clients at $1k+/mo before writing any "real" software.

## Key Takeaways

- **Build agents, don't be one.** Martell's S-tier stack (agent development → consulting → cybersecurity) all monetizes the same shift: SMBs replacing labor dollars with AI. The wedge that matters is *one use case, one vertical, pre-sold before built*.
- **Pre-sell beats pre-build.** Both Martell (waitlist → early-adopter payment links → concierge launch → productize last) and Wholesale Ted ($2.50 episodes before any scale) front-load validation at near-zero cost. Copy this sequencing everywhere.
- **AI content monetization still works where AI is the *method*, not the *product*** — language-lesson podcasts and niche novels package genuine utility (learning, genre fiction) with AI production economics. Pure "AI slop" (faceless montages) is F-tier per Martell and demonetization-prone.
- **Discovery graduation:** one genuinely new model entered the theme pool today — **managed AI cybersecurity for SMBs** (monthly-retainer AI threat monitoring; Martell: "wildly profitable, almost no competition, S tier"). Added queries: `how to start an AI cybersecurity business for small businesses` + `managed AI cybersecurity services monthly retainer`. Watch for a dedicated video in a future run.
