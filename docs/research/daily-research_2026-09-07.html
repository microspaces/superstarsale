The stunt: Koerner scraped ~500 tree-trimming businesses in Texas (Outscraper, ~$4), recorded an 18-second voicemail on his iPhone, and fired 475 ringless voicemail drops via Sly Broadcast - live, unedited, then answered the callbacks on his Google Voice number trying to sell $500-2K/month AI voice agents.

What actually happened (real numbers shown on screen):

  • 475 uploaded → Sly's filter killed 25 invalid → ~50 sends/minute → 282 successful (56%); DNC scrubbing is automatic
  • Key wrinkle: many "failed" sends still landed - two businesses that showed "failed: unable to detect voicemail" texted back within minutes (their own GHL automations caught the missed call). The channel is messier than the dashboard suggests, in both directions
  • Result: ~a dozen callbacks + inbound texts within the hour, including real conversations (one friendly hang-up after he offered to do it free, one booking-style exchange, one "STOP")
  • His own expectation, stated before sending: 1-8% callback rate, and only 20-60% of callers actually heard the VM - the rest are just returning a missed call. He names the mechanic "reverse cold calling" (he even grabbed the domain)

Why this works (the transferable insight): an engineered missed call flips the psychology of outreach - they call you, so the frame is inbound, not interruption. He's explicit this only works for offers where an inbound call has value to the receiver (home-services leads = yes; his RV-park acquisition outreach = no, a callback proves the number works, not interest). Standard multi-touch caveat: standalone RVM underperforms; stack it with text/email/call.

The build (second half): full GHL AI voice agent in ~25 minutes on stream - create agent → GPT-4o default → knowledge base generated by a ChatGPT prompt or the web crawler pointed at the business's own site (25K chars, PDFs, FAQs) → live test call handled scheduling + capture naturally and disclosed "I'm an AI" when asked → email notification on call completion → one-click agent templates + a marketplace to sell agents to GHL users.
