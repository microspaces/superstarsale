### 🏆 Top Automatable Business: Boring Niche Utility Websites
The highest automation potential combined with strong quality scores makes this the standout strategy. A single well-built tool (unit converter, calculator, template generator) can attract millions of organic visitors and generate entirely passive revenue through ads or subscriptions. Once products are created and uploaded, these platforms handle all fulfillment, customer service, and delivery. The key is niche selection and volume. Word search books and low-content journals remain underserved compared to standard lined notebooks.

### 🥉 Highest Income Potential: Growth Marketing
While requiring more active involvement in partnership building, the revenue-share model with successful creators can scale to significant monthly income. The AI-assisted product creation reduces the skill barrier dramatically.

- **Any model requiring your face/daily presence** limits true automation
