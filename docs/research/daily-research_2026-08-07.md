- Best for: Long-term wealth builders willing to reinvest

- **Pick ONE system and execute** — research paralysis kills more businesses than bad ideas

- **Build systems, not jobs** — if it requires your presence to make money, it's not automated
- **Reinvest profits** into additional assets that compound over time
- **Start this week** — the best business is the one you can actually start now
