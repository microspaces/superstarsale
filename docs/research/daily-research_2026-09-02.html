2. Vending Machine Route with Real Costs — Profitopsy

- Business model: Own vending machines placed in high-traffic third-party locations (factories, gyms, auto shops, apartment lobbies, busy offices); you keep them stocked and collect the sales; location may take a 0–25% commission.

- Revenue model: 50–100% item markup (buy snack ~$0.50 / sell $1.25–$1.50). A well-placed machine grosses ~$300–$500/month; after product (~40–50% of revenue), commission, fuel, and fees, net margin is 30–45% — roughly $140–$180/month on a $400 gross machine. Payback 16–17 months on a ~$2,500 all-in first machine. 10–20 machines ≈ $2–$3K/month net; ~50 machines is a full-time living.

- Steps to replicate:

1. Land a location before buying anything — get a verbal yes from one good spot (50+ captive people, no easy food nearby). This flips the risk: buy the machine for a real home, don't gamble on finding one.

2. One focused paperwork afternoon: LLC ($50–$500), free EIN, business bank account, general liability insurance (~$400–$700/yr), seller's permit / local vending license.

3. Buy one used combo machine (~$1,200–$3,000) with working bill validator + card reader (~$150). Realistic all-in first machine: ~$2,500 including first fill (~$350) and setup, plus a $300–$500 repair cushion.

4. Stock proven sellers matched to the location (gym = water/protein; factory = filling snacks/energy drinks); track sales data, cut losers, double winners. Price for convenience at ~50% margin — vending customers don't price-shop.

5. Reinvest profits machine-by-machine; cluster geography to cut drive time; at 20–30 machines hire part-time route help.

- Automation potential: 5/10 — Card readers + remote sales telemetry, route-sequencing, and a simple per-machine P&L sheet systematize the back office; but restocking, hauling, machine moves, and location deals are irreducibly physical/human.

- Quality: 7/10 — Best video of the day: complete cost stack, honest "not passive, year one is real work" framing, mistake list (cash-only, overstocking, keeping dead locations out of politeness), no course funnel, points to SBA/IRS resources. Deductions: all figures are estimates, no operator books shown, small channel.

- Verdict: ✅ Worth Trying (physical operators only) — Repeat theme: vending appeared as a list item on 07-09, 07-10, 07-11, 07-12, and 07-13, but always as one bullet in "passive income" roundups. This is the first dedicated deep dive, and its numbers confirm those earlier list claims ($300–$500/mo gross per machine). Novelty within the theme: medium.
