Licensed commercial grease trap (FOG) pumping route. UCO resale: bulk UCO spiked above $1,400/metric ton in early 2026 (SAF demand). Costs: used vacuum truck ~$60K; licensing stack $2K–7K over 6–16 weeks (USDOT $0, MC authority ~$300 + $25–50 agent, state EPD registration ~$200/yr, Class B CDL + tanker endorsement $1.5K–4K); market cited at $1.42B growing ~6%/yr.

Replication steps#↑ top

1. Buy a used vacuum truck (medium-duty chassis, 800–1,000 gal steel tank, mid-range pump) — ~$60K.

2. Stack the three licensing layers: USDOT (free) → motor carrier authority (~$300) → state transporter registration (pick a streamlined state: TX/FL/GA/TN/NC over NY/DE) → driver's Class B CDL with tanker endorsement ($1.5K–4K). Budget $2K–7K and 6–16 weeks.

3. Week 1: get 3 bids from rendering plants/FOG processors for a disposal contract; set up dispatch software (ServiceCore or Routific); open business checking.

4. Week 2: visit the local wastewater authority's FOG program coordinator to get on the approved-hauler list; walk 15–20 independent restaurants/day within 5–10 miles; pitch beating current rate by 10–15% with auto-filed compliance manifests on a quarterly recurring schedule.

5. Weeks 3–4: execute first services, issue manifests, collect referrals + Google reviews, cluster accounts, and drive to the ~20-account density threshold; sell collected UCO to renderers as the second revenue line.
