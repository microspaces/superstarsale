## 1) 7 Passive Income Ideas - How I Make $2,000+ Per Day!

**Channel:** Greg Gottfried | ~1,164K views | [https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=XFh3tRObiLM](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=XFh3tRObiLM)

### Business model
Roundup of 7 low-capital passive-income streams: high-yield savings interest, Amazon KDP low-content (word search) books, Amazon Merch on Demand print-on-demand tees, affiliate marketing, YouTube AdSense, Etsy digital downloads, and online courses on Udemy/Skillshare.

### Revenue model
Claims $800,000+ total passive income last year and $2,000+/day (title) across the streams — no per-business breakdown given. Only concrete stated rates: Capital One Performance 360 / Ally 3.8% and Betterment 4% savings interest; a "70% of internet traffic is video" stat to justify YouTube over blogs. No revenue numbers per model.

### Replication steps
1. Park cash in a high-yield online savings account (Capital One Performance 360, Ally, or Betterment) for automated interest.
2. Create word search puzzle books (the low-competition KDP niche he recommends), upload cover + interior files to Amazon KDP; Amazon handles printing, shipping, and customer service.
3. For Amazon Merch on Demand, run niche research to find small unsaturated niches, upload t-shirt designs; Amazon prints/ships on purchase (higher margins than KDP but more competition).
4. Do affiliate marketing via company referral programs — preferably as a YouTube channel with recommendation videos and referral links rather than a blog, since video outperforms articles.
5. Build a YouTube channel around educational content (advertisers pay more than for entertainment content); videos keep earning AdSense for 5–15 years.
6. Create a digital download that saves buyers time (e.g., wedding invitation templates, a pre-built budgeting spreadsheet) and list it on Etsy for organic marketplace traffic.
7. Package career or hobby expertise into a step-by-step course and sell it on Udemy or Skillshare (hardest — requires existing skills first).

### Automation: 7/10
KDP, Merch, digital downloads, and courses are upload-once models where the platform fulfills, collects payment, and handles support; savings interest is fully automatic. Manual residue: niche/design/content creation up front, and affiliate/YouTube income depends on ongoing publishing.

### Quality: 4/10
Receipts test: low. The $800K/yr and $2,000+/day claims are never backed by an earnings dashboard; his "real examples on my computer" are just public Amazon/Etsy listings, not his own revenue screens. Bank rates and business-model mechanics are verifiable and accurate, but every income claim is unverifiable.

**Verdict: Mixed — 5/10.**

### Key actions
- Start with KDP word-search books — the one concrete low-competition niche he names — and validate with a small catalog before scaling.
- If doing Merch on Demand, invest in niche research to avoid saturated big niches rather than chasing popular designs.
- Choose educational (not entertainment) YouTube content and attach affiliate links; it's the compounding asset in his list.

### Red flags
- Headline income claims ($800K/yr, $2,000+/day) shown with zero dashboards or verifiable proof.
- Every segment funnels viewers to his "free tutorials" in the description — the video itself is top of his marketing funnel.
- HYSA "passive income" requires existing capital; KDP/Merch returns per unit are tiny and the models are heavily saturated (he concedes Merch is more crowded than KDP).
- No per-business revenue figures anywhere, so expected returns can't be sanity-checked.
