### Video 1: Alex Hormozi — "Watch This To Generate 1000s of Leads (In Any Niche)" {#video-1}

> ▶ Watch: https://youtu.be/Mst4hreQYl0

**What it is:** Conversion-rate playbook, not a business: replace "book a call / submit for quote" CTAs with a mini-offer (lead magnet) solving a narrow problem, capturing contact info, funneling into the core paid offer. Hormozi sells the ecosystem via Acquisition.com.
**Core claims:** Magnet types ranked — (a) reveal-a-problem audits/tests (best: creates urgency), (b) free trial/limited sample, (c) one step of a multi-step process. Delivery vehicles: software/tool, info, done-for-you service, physical. Framework math: $25-cost magnet priced at ~$250 perceived value, 1-in-4 conversion ≈ $100 CAC; historical anecdote 213 members / $112K added to one gym via a free case-study magnet. Naming conventions (number + outcome + timeframe; "how to X without Y"), qualification gating so only ICP-quality leads get the good version, CTAs at the point of "greatest deprivation."
**Receipts vs self-reported:** Named businesses (Gym Launch, Acquisition.com) and a verifiable public track record; but the multipliers are directional, not measured — no dashboards.
**Funnel:** Acquisition.com portfolio/workshops/books.
**Verdict: Worth Trying — Q8, automation 7/10.** Assessment/scoring-style magnets are highly scriptable (website-audit pattern); free-service magnets are deliberately manual.
**Transferable:** Build one "reveal a problem" audit tool for the strongest niche in play — it doubles as lead gen and a capability demo. A/B test 3–6 headlines before building content (headline moves conversion more than contents). Gate with a qualification dropdown.

**Receipts:** Named, checkable track record (Yoyodyne→AOL sale, Akimbo B Corp, 19 bestsellers, specific historical dollar figures) — but 1980s–90s anecdotes, not current benchmarks. Zero replicable business mechanics.

**Transferable:** Ship one owned-channel piece daily for 90 days (permission, not followers). Audit the week: any task a competent freelancer could do = hire it out, starting with $20 tools. Write a one-page "smallest viable audience" definition.
