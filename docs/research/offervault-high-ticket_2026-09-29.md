## Fit notes for the Instagram-organic method

1. **Form-fill beats phone-call for organic.** Motley Fool-style lead gen converts from content; pay-per-call needs the
   viewer to dial and stay 90–180s. Reels drive clicks, not calls — Tier 2 call offers work best with a bridge page
   that offers "get a free quote/callback" so WE initiate the call.
2. **Best quality/effort ratio:** Home Security ($600, no buffer, social allowed, cap 100) and Solar ($40–$100,
   40 states, social allowed). Both map to huge homeowner content buckets the video describes.
3. **Mass tort / rehab / MVA** pay the most but are call-center quality: strict scripts, qualifying answers, low caps
   (MVA = 10/day). Not a beginner organic play; expect scrutiny from the network too.
4. **Compliance:** rehab/mental-health/legal = restricted categories on Meta; organic reels still can't make income or
   outcome claims. Always FTC-disclose on the bridge page (video's own rule) — never direct-link from IG bio.
5. **Content math (video's model):** Home Security at $600 ≈ one install/week = $2,400/mo; Solar at $70 avg needs
   ~5 qualified calls/day for $10K/mo. Both are content-bucket friendly (250-angles AI trick from the video).
