## Strategy Analysis

### 1. 10 Claude AI Side Hustles (Shane Hummus) — *AI services listicle*

- **Model:** ten "Claude Projects + questionnaire + human edit pass" service businesses (travel itineraries, dating profiles, YT scripts, ghostwriting, VSL copy, KDP books, course-as-a-service, newsletters, resumes). Price at 1/3–1/10 of agency rates, 24–48h delivery.
- **Numbers:** all secondhand/market-rate — $2–5K/mo ghostwriting retainers, $25–50K course builds, $500K/mo KDP client anecdote. No own receipts.
- **Automation:** 6/10 (half the list scriptable; the durable half needs the human edit pass he keeps stressing) · **Quality:** 4/10 · **Verdict: Mixed**
- **The two real picks:** B2B newsletter retainers and ATS resume optimization — validated demand, real AI leverage, boring enough to work.
- **Red flags:** the video is a lead magnet for his live training; no client-acquisition system taught (the hard part hand-waved); best-case framed as typical.
- **Note:** Amazon's 3-books/day KDP cap killed bulk-spam — the surviving play is one quality book, not volume.

### 2. One-Person AI Business (Richard Yu) — *digital products / value ladder*

- **Model:** $27–47 AI-drafted info product → AI storefront → short-form traffic → $2K coaching upsell (~3% conversion). By his own math 60%+ of revenue is the coaching rung — i.e., manual hours.
- **The one durable tactic:** outlier-post mining — find small accounts (10–50K followers) whose single post has 3–5× their follower count in views; that outlier is validated demand. Free, fast, transferable to any niche.
- **Automation:** 6/10 front-end, but the economics depend on non-automatable coaching · **Quality:** 3/10 · **Verdict: Mixed (leaning Skip)**
- **Red flags:** every dollar figure unverifiable ($80K/mo from an AI monk persona, $32M lifetime); "nobody is teaching this" is the oldest infomarketing model with AI speedruns; his own "one-person" framing collapses mid-pitch (75 staff admitted).

### 3. Pick a Profitable Agency Niche (The Futur) — *positioning strategy*

- **Model:** vertical-vs-horizontal niching + a 4-criteria scoring matrix (enjoyment, problem size, ability to pay, ease of conversation; >30/40 = has legs) + "date before marry" validation before rebranding.
- **Automation:** 3/10 · **Quality:** 7/10 — credible, established education business, sound doctrine, but zero revenue mechanics · **Verdict: Mixed**
- **Use:** the scoring matrix is a decent preprocessing step for any productized-service launch; nothing more.

### 4. Build and Sell AI Agents as a Beginner (Hostinger Academy) — *AI agency playbook*

- **Model:** n8n + LLM + CRM + Airtable agents (lead qualifier with website-scanning rubric, proposal generator, AI sales rep with memory) sold to admin-heavy local businesses: $2–5K per build, $5K+ multi-agent, retainers for hosting/monitoring. ROI pricing script: 50 leads/wk × 20 min × $30/hr ≈ $25K/yr labor → "$10K project fee."
- **Automation:** 8/10 — the deliverables ARE automations; build once, clone per client · **Quality:** 5/10 — concrete reproducible workflows (free JSONs provided), zero practitioner receipts, and it's a Hostinger VPS ad · **Verdict: Mixed**
- **Key actions:** import the lead-qualifier workflow and get one end-to-end run on your own data; record a 3-minute Loom triaging a real prospect's public form submission; lead the pitch with their own labor math.
- **Red flags:** most-saturated AI-agency pitch of 2024–26; "zero coding experience" hook unverified; sales skill is the real gate and it's not taught.
