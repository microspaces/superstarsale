## 1. The buyer (page built around THEM, not the topic)
- Who: 22–40, side-hustle-curious, burned by "start a course" advice; wants a small win this week, not a business degree.
- Fears: "I'm not an expert", "nobody will pay for a spreadsheet", "it'll take months".
- Buying triggers: cheap ($7–12 = impulse), instant download, solves a chore they already have TODAY (budget, meals, first client).
- Page identity: **Boring Business Club** — bio: "Boring files that quietly pay. Templates · trackers · checklists. Free starter checklist ↓" — link = bridge page (never direct store links in bio/captions; disclosure + context live on the page).

## 2. Content system — 8 buckets, 80 hooks
Four-part reel: **Hook → Value → Tease → CTA.** Trigger word: **CHECKLIST**.
- Bucket A — "watch me build one" (speedrun builds, screen-record): hooks 1–10
- Bucket B — the chore is the enemy (relatable pain: bills surprise, Sunday scaries, client email ping-pong): 11–20
- Bucket C — proof/screenshotPOV (sales pings, download stats, "this file paid for groceries"): 21–30
- Bucket D — 5-products listicle format (the reel's own format — clone it): 31–40
- Bucket E — "boring > shiny" contrarian (no course, no ads, no personal brand): 41–50
- Bucket F — how-to-use teardown (90 seconds inside one file, show ONE formula/slide): 51–60
- Bucket G — niche swaps (same file for nurses, realtors, gym coaches — new audience each): 61–70
- Bonus (storytime/POV/comparisons): 71–80

Rule: same niche, different angle. When a reel hits, double down on that format.

## 6. Compliance guardrails
- No income claims. "Sold while you sleep" only with a real receipt on screen; otherwise "quietly sell" framing without numbers. Never "passive income guaranteed".
- Budget/tax files = organizers, not financial/tax advice — the page says so; captions don't give advice.
- Own-store disclosure lives on the bridge page ("links go to our own store, not a sponsor").
- Meta ToS: engagement-bait phrasing ("comment CHECKLIST") is tolerated for now but keep the value in the reel itself; no reward-for-engagement language.
- Verify any stat used in a hook before that reel posts. Drop or hedge unverified claims.
