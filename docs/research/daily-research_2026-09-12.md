## 1. Best Places to Buy Affiliate Websites — Matt Diggity (4,016 views)

- **Business model:** Buy existing affiliate/content sites (marketplaces + off-market "starter sites" via FB groups), ride existing SEO authority.
- **Revenue model:** Marketplaces price at 30-40x monthly profit ($1K/mo site ≈ $35K). Claims a $1.5K starter-site buy now earns $25K/mo (unverified).
- **Replication steps:**
  1. Buy vetted sites (Empire Flippers, FE International, Investors.club); min ~$15K entry.
  2. Cheaper path: hunt "starter sites" (content, no earnings) in FB groups.
  3. Price starters on content volume + potential; use seller's sunk-cost emotion in negotiation.
  4. Vet backlinks/seller; scale via acquisition, not fresh builds.
- **Automation score:** 3/10 — SEO/content ops stay manual.
- **Quality score:** 6/10 — real marketplaces, standard multiples, but 1.5K→25K claim unverifiable.
- **Verdict:** Mixed
- **Key actions:**
  - First buy via vetted marketplace to learn escrow/transfers.
  - Source cheaper deals in FB groups; $500/mo min earnings filter.
  - Due-diligence backlinks before wiring funds.
- **Red flags:** Survivorship bias; "potential-based" starter-site pricing is speculative; marketplace minimums exclude beginners.

## 3. How To Buy The Right Content Websites — Jaryd Krause (183 views)

- **Business model:** Buy under-monetized content sites with easy SEO wins, fix monetization/traffic, hold or flip. Same acquisition thesis as #1.
- **Revenue model:** First site $900 (~$30/mo); ~14 sites bought ($12K elevations site, $14K ceiling-fans site); claims 100% ROI on one (unverified).
- **Replication steps:**
  1. Niche filter: content sites only; exclude FBA/apps.
  2. 10-point checklist; demand Google Analytics access.
  3. Screen ~10 min: reject bad traffic geo (China/India kills ad RPM).
  4. Buy cheap first ($500-1K) to learn escrow/transfers; scale up.
  5. Swap AdSense for premium ad networks for quick margin.
- **Automation score:** 2/10 — diligence + SEO fixes are hands-on.
- **Quality score:** 5/10 — purchase prices named; earnings outcomes anecdotal.
- **Verdict:** Mixed
- **Key actions:**
  - Start with one sub-$1K site; learn escrow, domain/analytics/AdSense transfers.
  - Build a 10-question screen; check traffic geo before price talks.
  - Target under-monetized sites for ad-network swap arbitrage.
- **Red flags:** Survivorship bias ("exploded, no work of my own"); no earnings proof beyond purchase prices.

## 4. How to Sell Software to Create $10k/Month Passive Income — Andy Audate (83 views)

- **Business model:** Recruit affiliates for "Progeta," a white-label all-in-one CRM/agency platform (GoHighLevel-style). You refer local businesses via a personal landing page + pre-recorded demo; vendor does onboarding/retention/billing.
- **Revenue model:** 50% recurring commission: $200/mo per $399 Platinum customer, $374/mo per $749 Diamond. Claims 26 Diamond or ~50 Platinum customers = $10k/mo. Zero proof of actual affiliate earnings shown.
- **Replication steps:**
  1. Join 50/50 affiliate program, get tracked landing page.
  2. Pitch local business owners at networking events / warm network.
  3. Send them to page with pre-recorded demo; vendor closes, onboards, retains.
  4. Stack recurring commissions month over month.
- **Automation score:** 6/10 — vendor handles fulfillment; your acquisition is manual networking/cold outreach.
- **Quality score:** 2/10 — a recruiting pitch for the program itself, not a case study. No dashboards, no named earning affiliates, arithmetic-only income claims.
- **Verdict:** Skip — you'd be the product (an unpaid salesforce) for a $400/mo CRM whose retention/commission terms are unverified and revocable by the vendor.
- **Key actions:**
  - If curious, demand the affiliate agreement: clawback, term, and payout history before recruiting anyone.
  - Same economics exist via GoHighLevel's own $497 SaaS plan (keep 100%, proven playbook).
- **Red flags:** Podcast exists to recruit affiliates, not teach; "Brian" anecdote unverifiable; 95% churn-free retention claim unsupported; single-vendor dependency; classic guru funnel.
