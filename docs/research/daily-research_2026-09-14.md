**Transferable:** Treat it as "YouTube management" (budget for editor/thumbnails), not pure automation. Choose niche for CPM + trend growth before making anything.

## Key Takeaways

- **No new model candidates** — faceless channels, productized consulting, creator monetization, and education content are all already in the theme pool. Today was a refinement day, not a discovery day.
- **Standardization was the common thread:** every video argued for narrow, repeatable offers (faceless-channel SOPs, named consultant frameworks, fixed-price packages). The bottleneck each one admits: niche judgment and trust still require humans.
- **Evidence hierarchy held again:** the video with checkable facts (Two Teachers, Q7) sold nothing; the ones with big unverifiable numbers ($110K AdSense, $10M productized, $40K email) all funnel to paid coaching. Discount dollar figures, keep frameworks.
- **Best extractable tactic:** pre-sell the outline before building (Video 3) — zero-cost validation applicable to any offer on the board.
- **Faceless-channel reality check (Video 1):** the honest data point is the failure set — one ~$3K exit, a demonetized 12M-view compilation channel, and non-repeating virality. If we ever test this lane, budget for human editors and avoid reused-content formats.
