## Key Takeaways

### For Automatable Businesses:

1. **Micro-SaaS is the clear winner.** A single-purpose tool that solves a painful data problem can generate $40K/month with one person and 97% profit margins. The key is finding a narrow niche (PDF conversion, data extraction, format translation) and building the simplest possible MVP.

2. **AI has made product creation trivial.** Tools like Synthesize AI and Ghostwriter OS can generate digital products in minutes. The new bottleneck is distribution, not production. Partnering with creators who have audiences but no products is a leveraged play.

3. **Evergreen funnels beat live sales for automation.** If you're selling anything above $500, record a webinar once and let it sell 24/7. Combine with email automation and retargeting ads to create a machine that works while you sleep.

4. **Skip labor-heavy models unless you want a job.** Outbound sales, 1:1 coaching, and manual content creation are profitable but not automatable. If your goal is passive or semi-passive income, prioritize software and digital products over services.

5. **Organic > Paid for bootstrapped founders.** Angus spent $1,000 on ads to make $300 in sales. He turned off ads, focused on product quality, and grew to $40K MRR through organic search. For solo founders, SEO and word-of-mouth are the most sustainable growth channels.
