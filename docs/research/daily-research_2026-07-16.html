Key Takeaways

For Building Automatable Businesses:

1. Micro-SaaS is the #1 automation play. Angus Chang's $40K/month solo business proves that a single-feature app, once built, generates revenue with near-zero ongoing labor. With AI coding tools, the barrier to entry is lower than ever.

2. The "Stacking" principle wins. Harry's research shows the path: start small (ATM/digital product), reinvest profits, stack systems. $300/month → $3K/month → $30K/month through compound asset acquisition.

3. AI is the great equalizer for creator partnerships. Iman's model leverages AI to do the "heavy lifting" of product creation, allowing non-experts to build and launch digital products for creators. The automation is in the AI fulfillment, not the human labor.

4. Avoid time-for-money traps. High-ticket sales and creative services can generate income quickly but create a ceiling. Use them as FUEL, not the destination. Every hour spent on a sales call is an hour NOT spent building a system.

5. Organic > Paid for sustainable SaaS. Angus tried Google Ads, cold email, and blogging — only organic search and product improvement worked. Focus on solving a real problem; the users will find you.
