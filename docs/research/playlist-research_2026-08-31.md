## Video 1: Nick Ponte - "I Tested the NEW Ways to Make Money With AI"

**Story:** Maui agency owner argues the 2026 opportunity isn't selling "AI" - it's selling simple **outcomes** to small businesses. Flagship: "social posting as a service" - reviving dormant small-business social profiles with AI-generated posts, sold as $500-$3,000/mo subscriptions after a free 2-week sample. Full client-acquisition funnel demoed inside GoHighLevel.

**Mechanics:**
- Pricing tiers: $500/mo = 1 platform, $1,000/mo = 2 platforms; community reports up to $2,000/mo for daily posting to 3 platforms; one member does $150/mo for 2 short videos/week
- Lead gen: Gemini prompt builds list of 20 local businesses with 500-1,000 followers who haven't posted in 6+ months ("do not hallucinate businesses"), manually verified on Facebook
- Fulfillment: GoHighLevel Social Planner - AI post text + AI images; ~10 min to create 2 weeks of content; ~1 hour/month to schedule; VA in Philippines runs it (AI writes English, native fluency no longer required)
- Key scope reframe: selling *posting* (activity), NOT marketing (results) - "The posts do not have to have thousands of likes... they just have to make the profile look active"
- His claimed revenue: ~$123K last month from small-business services (QuickBooks screenshot, unaudited); 60K free community, 500+ paid
- Upsell ladder: free 2 weeks → posting subscription → website rebuilds, CRM work

**Automation angle:** Highly automatable end-to-end (lead lists, dormant-profile detection, generation, scheduling, outreach cadences). Human: verification, sales calls, QC, relationship upsells.

**Red flags:** Self-reported revenue; video is itself a GoHighLevel-affiliate funnel; "97% know / 3% implemented" stat unsourced; churn risk when clients ask "where are the customers?" is never addressed.

# Cross-Playlist Synthesis

## What this playlist actually is

An accidental but coherent curriculum: **four local-AI-service agency models** (Ponte, Hummus #4, GHL Wizard, Koerner/Stewart), **four agent-platform operator demos** (McCoy, Brown, Ai Untapped, Goldie), **two product-business teardowns** (Welltory, Fastlane), and **two creator-funnel playbooks** (Hummus #2, Yu). Same shape repeats: person uses AI to collapse a service's delivery cost, sells the outcome on recurring, and the video itself is the top of their own funnel.

## Repeatable patterns (appear in 3+ videos)

1. **Demo-first selling** - build the free sample before the pitch (Ponte's 2 weeks of content, Hummus's brain-in-a-box demo, GHL's customized demo site, Fastlane's incentivized calls). The demo IS the sales call.
2. **Recurring > one-time** - every agency model converges on monthly retainers ($150-$3,000/mo band; Koerner's law: "if you solve a problem one time, you get paid one time").
3. **Value-based pricing, never hourly** - Hummus states it outright; Ponte's tiers and Welltory's $99.99/yr are priced on outcome, not effort.
4. **One human gate** - McCoy's editors-before-publish, Goldie's approve/reject step, Brown's PR-screenshot review, Ai Untapped's paper-trading constraint. Even the most automation-forward operators keep exactly one human checkpoint - and say so explicitly.
5. **Voice/story capture as the moat** - Fieldy wearable, Voice DNA interview, Fastlane's 2,000 calls, Zach's 30-years-of-trades interview. The data nobody else has is the founder's own words; agents do the packaging.
6. **Owned audience over rented platforms** - Yu's ManyChat DM capture, Hummus's "mother platform," McCoy's email lists, Stewart's podcast RSS. Same line everywhere: you're renting.

## Meta red flags (whole playlist)

- **Every video is a funnel.** Free guide → community → masterclass → coaching, in all 12. The advice is the marketing; apply the Mom Test to the advisors themselves.
- **All revenue numbers are self-reported.** $123K/mo, $32M lifetime, $18M ARR, $69K MRR, $3M/yr - zero audited. Treat every figure as an upper bound.
- **Survivorship everywhere.** No failure cases in any video; the honest operators (Welltory, Fastlane) are the ones admitting years of failed onboarding and 1,000+ calls of grind.
- **The genuinely load-bearing advice is boring and repeated:** talk to customers, sell outcomes on recurring, keep one human gate, own your audience, stay consistent. The AI wrapper changes the cost curve, not the laws.
