## Key Takeaways

1. **Programmatic SEO remains the top automation play** — the boring-tool-site model (9/10) is fully systemizable: AI page generation, static hosting, ad/network revenue, zero fulfillment. The only inputs are niche selection and patience through a 6-12 month ranking window.
2. **"AI does the work" is now the standard pitch — discount accordingly.** Video 2's creator-partnership model is genuinely semi-automatable (7/10) but wrapped in a self-serving funnel (presenter owns the marketplace AND the coaching upsell). Extract the model, ignore the ecosystem.
3. **Rev-share beats flat fees for automation economics.** Both Video 2 (30% of creator launches) and Video 4's self-storage (14-month average tenancy) show recurring/persistent revenue is what makes a system "sleep-income" rather than a project.
4. **Conversion-rate constants for planning:** 1-3% (email), 0.01-1% (video/social). Any audience-monetization forecast that ignores these is fiction — use them to size offers and price products.
5. **Skip anything where the deliverable is your live time** — high-ticket closing (2/10) is the anti-pattern: high income ceiling, zero leverage, and today's example was also the lowest-credibility video of the set.
6. **The ladder concept is sound even when the rungs aren't:** start with a capital-light automated asset (digital product/POD), stack cash flow, reinvest into bigger systems. Keep the framework, choose laptop-native rungs.
