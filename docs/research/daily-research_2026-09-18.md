### 2. Reselling 101: Thrift Store Flipping for Beginners — FlippyMcFlipperson

- **Business model:** Buy undervalued goods at thrift stores (Goodwill/Salvation Army), resell on eBay.
- **Revenue model:** Example: $5 Honey thermostat → sold $58 in 2 days (~$50 gross margin after fees). No aggregate income figures given.
- **Replication steps:**
  1. Pick a niche you know (hobby expertise = edge).
  2. Research sellable brands via YouTube/Instagram resellers; build a lookout list.
  3. Map stores via Google Maps + route planner; plan a loop.
  4. In-store: scan barcodes/prices in the eBay app; check sold comps + sell-through rate (sold > listed = fast mover).
  5. Set a personal minimum margin per item (fee-aware).
  6. List with clean photos, averaged sold-comp pricing, full item specifics.
  7. Track inventory with eBay custom SKUs + bin system; bookkeep from day one (spreadsheet or Reseller Genie).
  8. Ship over-packed; print labels via eBay.
- **Automation potential:** 2/10 — sourcing and listing are inherently manual; only bookkeeping/listing helpers automate.
- **Quality:** 6/10 — concrete item, price, and sell-through method shown; single data point, no dashboard.
- **Verdict:** Mixed — real, low-capital, but time-heavy and scaling-capped.
- **Key actions:** Start in a known hobby niche; enforce a sell-through-rate filter; set a $/hour floor.
- **Red flags:** Margins die on fees at low price points; income claims vague ("pretty good money"); affiliate pitch for Reseller Genie.
- **Novelty:** none (flipping).
